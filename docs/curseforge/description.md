# Famulus

**Your companion for playing together.**

World of Warcraft is at its best with other people: the friend who levels alongside you, the
guildmate who is always up for one more dungeon, the stranger from last night's group who turned out
to be great company. Famulus helps you stay close to them. Made for World of Warcraft: Forever.

*Famulus is Latin for the assistant of a scholar or a magician.*

> **Beta:** Famulus is young and still growing. Expect rough edges, and please tell me in the
> comments what you run into and what you would like to see. Something not working? Type

> [GitHub](https://github.com/89pleasure/famulus/issues). It holds no names.

## Chronicle: your journey as a picture book

Famulus records what happens on your adventures and tells it as a picture book, one chapter per
play session: levels, deaths, bosses, challenges, newly discovered zones, professions, abilities
like riding or what your class quests teach, and special items. Beat a boss as a group, and the chronicle remembers who was there.

- **Share it** with friends and guild members who use Famulus too, no matter where they are. News
  even reaches you through mutual friends while someone is offline.
- **React** to your friends' moments: cheer a level-up, mourn a death, applaud a profession.
- **Live cards on screen** when your friends level up, fall or beat a boss.
- **A side column** with your week in numbers, your professions, and your friends with their level
  and online status.

## Fellow travellers: remember who you played with

Famulus remembers everyone you grouped with: when and where you met, how often, for how long, and
which bosses you beat together. The more time you spend together, the stronger your bond, from
*Encounter* to *Acquainted*, *Trusted* and *Inseparable*. Click someone to see your story together, or whisper them with one click.
If you like, it keeps only friends and guild members, and tidying up removes everyone else in one go.
All of this stays on your computer and is never sent anywhere.

**Who is nearby:** see which of your friends and guild members are in your zone right now, with
their level, and get a card when one of them arrives. Nothing is sent, and they need no Famulus.

## Aliases: know who's who

Give other players names of your own that only you see.

- In chat, with the real name in brackets if you like: "Alias (Name)"
- As a line in the player tooltip
- Set straight from the right-click menu of a player or a Battle.net friend
- One alias for a Battle.net friend covers all of their characters

Whispers, invites and links keep using the real name.

## Getting started

Type **/fam** (or /famulus), click the minimap button or use the addon menu at the minimap. The
first time, a welcome page switches on the main features with one click, or lets you choose
yourself. The search finds every setting. Five colour themes; English, German, French, Spanish and
Italian.

## Light on your game

- No libraries, no dependencies.
- Anything that listens to the game or sends messages to other players starts switched off. You
  decide what runs.
- A feature that is switched off costs nothing, and nothing runs every frame while you are idle.
- Every setting applies instantly, no /reload needed.
- The window is only built when you open it for the first time.

## Privacy

The chronicle only talks to people on your friends list or in your guild who use Famulus
themselves, and only once you switch sharing on. Your fellow travellers and aliases never leave
your computer.
