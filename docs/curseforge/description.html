WoW Forever is all about spending time in Azeroth together: the friend who levels alongside you, the guildmate who is always up for one more dungeon, the stranger from last night's group who turned out to be great company. Famulus makes that time even better.

It is not another UI suite: it adds three things for playing together and leaves the rest of your interface alone.

  • Chronicle: your and your friends' journey as a picture book, with live cards when they level or beat a boss
  • Fellowship: everyone you grouped with, your bond with them, and who is nearby right now
  • Aliases: your own names for other players, in chat and tooltips

Made for World of Warcraft: Forever. See it in action, with demos you can try in your browser, on famulus.camp.

Beta: Famulus is young and still growing. Expect rough edges, and please tell me in the comments what you run into and what you would like to see. Something not working? Type /fam report, copy the text with Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac) and paste it into a comment or an issue on GitHub. The report contains no player names.

 

Famulus records what happens on your adventures and tells it as a picture book, one chapter per play session: levels, deaths, bosses, challenges, newly discovered zones, professions, abilities like riding or the ones your class quests teach, and special items. Beat a boss as a group, and the chronicle remembers who was there.

  • Share it with friends and guild members who use Famulus too, no matter where they are. If a friend is offline when you log in, a mutual friend's Famulus passes on what you missed.
  • React to your friends' moments: cheer a level-up, mourn a death, applaud a profession.
  • A side column with your week in numbers, your professions, and your friends with their level and online status.

Live feed: a card on screen when a friend levels up, falls or beats a boss, and you react with one click. Older cards move down as slim rows, and friends who beat a boss together share one card. You choose where it sits and which events it shows.

 

Famulus remembers everyone you grouped with: when and where you met, how often, for how long, and which bosses you beat together. The more time you spend together, the stronger your bond, from Encounter to Acquainted, Trusted and Inseparable. Optionally keep only friends and guild members; one click clears out everyone else. Battlegrounds and arenas never count. All of this stays on your computer and is never sent anywhere.

Click someone to see your story together, or whisper them with one click.

Who is nearby: see which of your friends and guild members are in your zone right now, with their level, and get a card when one of them arrives. Friends on another realm or faction are listed apart. Nothing is sent, and they need no Famulus.

 

Your tank plays as "Grimtusk", but to you he's Tom. Give other players names of your own that only you see:

  • In chat, with the real name in brackets if you like: "Alias (Name)"
  • As a line in the player tooltip
  • Set straight from the right-click menu of a player or a Battle.net friend
  • One alias for a Battle.net friend covers all of their characters

Whispers, invites and links keep using the real name.

 

• Getting started

Type /fam (or /famulus), click the minimap button or use the addon menu on the minimap. The first time, a welcome page switches on the main features with one click, or lets you choose yourself. The search finds every setting. Five colour schemes; English, German, French, Spanish and Italian.

• Light on your game

  • No libraries, no dependencies.
  • Anything that listens to the game or sends messages to other players starts switched off. You decide what runs.
  • A feature that is switched off costs nothing, and nothing runs every frame while you are idle.
  • Every setting applies instantly, no /reload needed.
  • The window is only built when you open it for the first time.

• Privacy

The chronicle only talks to people on your friends list or in your guild who use Famulus themselves, and only once you switch sharing on. Your fellowship and aliases never leave your computer. More in the privacy policy.
