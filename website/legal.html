Privacy

As of 27 November 2026

In short: there are no accounts, and I store nothing about you. This website sets no cookies, has no analytics or tracking and loads nothing from other servers. Its host, GitHub, logs your IP address; the language and the scene you choose are remembered on your device only.

Who is responsible

89pleasure, the developer of Famulus. Email: 89pleasure@proton.me. You can also reach me on GitHub, at github.com/89pleasure; issues there are public.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver a page, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual details of a request, such as the page, the time and your browser's identification. GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes (GitHub's documentation). I have no access to these logs and receive no visitor statistics.

The legal basis is the legitimate interest in delivering this website reliably and securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub also processes data in the USA and is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its counterparts for Switzerland and the United Kingdom. Details are in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Stored in your browser

When you switch the language or choose a scene for the picture at the top, the page remembers your choice in your browser's local storage (famulus-lang, famulus-scene), so that your next visit opens with it. It stays on your device and is never sent. You can delete it in your browser's settings at any time. The page stores nothing else and sets no cookies; what you type into its demos, such as an alias, is neither sent nor kept.

Nothing from other servers

Fonts, images and scripts are delivered with the website itself. There is no analytics, no advertising, no social media plugin and no embedded content from other services. Links to GitHub and CurseForge only take you there when you follow them; from then on, their privacy policies apply.

The addon

Famulus, the addon, sends nothing to me and contains no tracking. What it remembers (chronicle, fellow travellers, aliases) stays in your game folder on your computer. What it shares in the game goes only through the game itself to friends and guild members who use Famulus too, and only once you switch sharing on.

No accounts, nothing to delete

There are no accounts and no registration, neither on this website nor in the addon, and I store no personal data about you. So there is nothing I could look up, correct or delete for you. The only data are GitHub's server logs (see above); requests about them go to GitHub. You can still complain to a data protection supervisory authority, or write to me at 89pleasure@proton.me.

In deinem Browser gespeichert

Wenn du die Sprache wechselst oder eine Szene für das Bild oben wählst, merkt sich die Seite deine Wahl im lokalen Speicher deines Browsers (famulus-lang, famulus-scene), damit dein nächster Besuch damit öffnet. Famulus, das Addon, schickt mir nichts und enthält kein Tracking. Was es sich merkt (Chronik, Weggefährten, Aliase), bleibt in deinem Spielordner auf deinem Rechner. Was es im Spiel teilt, geht nur über das Spiel selbst an Freunde und Gildenmitglieder, die Famulus auch nutzen, und erst, wenn du das Teilen einschaltest.
