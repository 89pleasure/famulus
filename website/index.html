01Chronicle

Your journey, told like a picture book.

Level-ups, deaths, defeated bosses, new zones, professions and special finds: the chronicle records what you and your friends go through, one chapter per play session.

  • Shared with your friends

    Friends and guildmates with Famulus share their milestones with you, wherever they are in Azeroth, and you react with one click. Friends without it still show up when they level.

  • Offline, but not left out

    If a friend is offline when you log in, a mutual friend's Famulus passes on what you missed.

  • You decide what is written

    Recording and sharing stay off until you switch them on, the first time with one click. Nothing goes beyond your friends list and guild.

02Fellow travellers

Remember who was there.

Famulus remembers everyone you grouped with: when and where you met, how often and how long you played together, and which bosses fell along the way.

Try it: a click on a tile or a name opens your story with that person; the speech bubble opens a whisper.

  • A bond that grows

    From Encounter to Inseparable: four tiers by the time you spent together. The ring around the crest shows how far it is to the next.

  • Who is nearby

    See which friends and guildmates are in your zone right now, and whisper them with one click. They don't need Famulus for it.

  • It stays with you

    Famulus only starts remembering once you switch it on, and sends none of it anywhere. Battlegrounds and arenas never count.

03Live feed

Be there when it happens.

When a friend beats a boss or learns to ride, a card pops up on your screen for a few seconds, and you can react with one click. You choose where it sits, how long it stays and which events it shows, your own included.

04Aliases

To you, Grimtusk is just Tom.

Your tank plays as “Grimtusk”, but to you he's Tom. Give players an alias and Famulus shows it in chat and in tooltips. Only you see it; whispers, invites and links still reach the character.

    06Installation

    Ready in a minute.

    1. 1

      Install it

      Get Famulus with the CurseForge app, or put the folder Famulus into World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/.

    2. 2

      Start the game

      If it was already running, restart it so that it finds the new folder.

    3. 3

      Open the window

      /fam opens Famulus. The flame button on the minimap does the same. The first time, a welcome page switches on the main features with one click.

    Getting started

    Does Famulus work in Retail or Classic?

    No. Famulus is made specifically for World of Warcraft: Forever (version 1.60), and that is the only place it is thoroughly tested.

    How do I open it?

    You can open Famulus any time with the flame logo on the minimap. If that ever doesn't work, you can always type /fam in chat.

    Why is my chronicle empty?

    Check whether you have switched recording off. Also, something has to happen first: Famulus doesn't fill in anything from the past.

    Which languages does it speak?

    English, German, French, Spanish and Italian. It follows your game's language; you can choose another one in its settings.

    Friends & privacy

    Do my friends need Famulus too?

    You can start on your own, but Famulus is at its best when your friends use it too. Then their milestones land in your chronicle, and the live feed always shows you what is happening with your friends right now. With just one click you can react to it. Without Famulus, there is less to see. Features like the fellow travellers, Nearby and the aliases work whether your friends use Famulus or not.

    What does Famulus send, and to whom?

    Only the chronicle shares, once you switch sharing on: your milestones and reactions go to friends and guildmates who use Famulus too. Nothing goes beyond your friends list and guild. The fellow travellers and the aliases send nothing.

    Can others see the aliases I give?

    No. Only you see the aliases you give. Unless, of course, you share a screenshot or happen to be streaming. So be careful what you call your “friends” ;)

    In the game

    How do I move the live feed?

    The classic way, with Blizzard's Edit Mode. Or type /fam feed and drag the marked box to where the cards should appear; /fam feed again fixes it, /fam feed reset puts it back. How long the cards stay and which events they show, your own included, you choose in the settings.

    Will it slow down my game?

    Famulus is developed with care and goes easy on your computer's resources. Should you ever feel that it slows your game down, please open a ticket on GitHub, the developer portal.

    Does it get along with other addons?

    Famulus adds to Blizzard's interface without replacing anything and leaves other addons alone. If something clashes anyway, open an issue on GitHub.

    Something not working?
