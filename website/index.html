01Aliases

Call them by the names you know.

Behind every character name is someone you call something else. Give players an alias and Famulus shows it in chat and in tooltips. Only you see it; whispers, invites and links still reach the character.

    In the game
    GeneralCombat log

    Try it: give Yorick Talwind an alias, change one, or switch the display under Settings in the sidebar.

    • One alias for the whole account

      Enter a friend's BattleTag and the alias covers all of their characters. Famulus remembers each one they come online with.

    • A right-click is enough

      “Set alias” is in the context menu of players and Battle.net friends. No list to open, no name to type.

    • Nothing gets overwritten

      The alias only changes what you read. Links keep the real name, and Blizzard's interface stays untouched.

    02Chronicle

    Your journey, told like a picture book.

    Level-ups, deaths, defeated bosses, new zones, professions and special finds: the chronicle records what you and your companions go through, one chapter per day.

    • Famulus talks to Famulus

      Friends who use the addon share their milestones with you, wherever in the world they are. Only with people on your friends list or in your guild.

    • Offline, but not left out

      If you were not online, a mutual companion passes on what you missed.

    • You decide what is written

      Recording and sharing are off until you switch them on. Companions appear under the alias you gave them.

    04Under the hood

    Quiet until you need it.

    Famulus is built to stay out of the way. What is switched off does not even listen to the game, so it costs nothing.

    • 0

      game events Famulus listens to after login with default settings

    • 0

      lines that run per frame while idle: no OnUpdate, no timer

    • 0

      libraries and dependencies; the window is only built when you open it

    • 2

      languages built in, English and German, switchable without a reload

    • Everything applies at once

      Every option, the language and the character profile take effect immediately. No /reload.

    • Nothing is replaced

      Famulus only hooks in. It never overwrites functions of the game or of other addons, which keeps it compatible.

    • Errors stay small

      An error switches off only the feature it happened in. If the game lacks something, the feature shows as unavailable.

    • Your data stays with you

      Nobody but you sees your aliases. Only what you switch on is shared, and only with friends and guild.

    Five colour schemes

    A colour scheme tints the whole window: surfaces, switches and highlights. Pick one, and this page follows.

    05Installation

    Ready in a minute.

    1. 1

      Copy the folder

      Put the folder Famulus into World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/.

    2. 2

      Start the game

      If it is already running, type /reload into the chat.

    3. 3

      Open the window

      /fam opens Famulus. The flame at the minimap does the same.
