An addon for World of Warcraft: Forever

For everyone you play with.

Famulus records your shared journey as a picture book, remembers who you travelled with and gives your friends the names you know them by.

01Chronicle

Your journey, told like a picture book.

Level-ups, deaths, defeated bosses, new zones, professions and special finds: the chronicle records what you and your companions go through, one chapter per day.

  • Famulus talks to Famulus

    Friends who use the addon share their milestones with you, wherever in the world they are. Only with people on your friends list or in your guild.

  • Offline, but not left out

    If you were not online, a mutual companion passes on what you missed.

  • You decide what is written

    Recording and sharing are off until you switch them on. Companions appear under the alias you gave them.

02Fellow travellers

Remember who was there.

Famulus remembers who you were in a group with: when and where you met, how often and how long you travelled together, and which bosses fell along the way. The others don't need Famulus for it.

Try it: a click on a tile or a name opens your story with that person.

  • A bond that grows

    From Encounter to Inseparable: four tiers by the time you spent together. The ring around the crest shows how far it is to the next.

  • Your story, day by day

    Every person has a book of your days together: where you were, for how long, which bosses fell and when you met.

  • It stays with you

    Famulus only starts remembering once you switch it on, and sends none of it anywhere. Battlegrounds and arenas never count.

03Live feed

Be there when it happens.

When a companion defeats a boss or learns to ride, a card appears on your screen for a few seconds. Where it sits, how long it stays and which events it shows, your own too if you like, is up to you.

04Aliases

Call them by the names you know.

Behind every character name is someone you call something else. Give players an alias and Famulus shows it in chat and in tooltips. Only you see it; whispers, invites and links still reach the character.

    Aliases in chat

    Shows the alias instead of the character name in front of chat messages. Whispers and invites still go to the character.

    Alias in the tooltip

    Adds a line with the alias to the tooltip of a player.

    Alias in the context menu

    Right-clicking a player or Battle.net friend offers to set, change or remove their alias.

    In the game
    GeneralCombat log

    Try it: give Yorick Talwind an alias, change one, or switch the display under Settings in the sidebar.

    • One alias for the whole account

      Enter a friend's BattleTag and the alias covers all of their characters. Famulus remembers each one they come online with.

    • A right-click is enough

      “Set alias” is in the context menu of players and Battle.net friends. No list to open, no name to type.

    • Nothing gets overwritten

      The alias only changes what you read. Links keep the real name, and Blizzard's interface stays untouched.

    05Installation

    Ready in a minute.

    1. 1

      Copy the folder

      Put the folder Famulus into World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/.

    2. 2

      Start the game

      If it was already running, restart it so that it finds the new folder.

    3. 3

      Open the window

      /fam opens Famulus. The flame at the minimap does the same.
